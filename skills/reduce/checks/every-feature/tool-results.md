# A tool result

**Signal.** The scope step gave a tool result to this feature.

**Decide with.** Trace each result in the feature's source. Close each one as a finding, a `keep` with its reason, or a rejection with its reason.
A score, an empty scan, or a report from another revision is not evidence of safety or completion.

**Keeps it when.** The trace shows that the reported pattern is needed, or that the tool's blind spot hides a user. See the tool limits in [static dead-code tools](../../evidence/tools-and-evidence.md#static-dead-code-tools).
