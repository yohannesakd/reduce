# A plan unit that no requirement needs

**Signal.** A plan unit, phase, migration, rollout step, or recovery mechanism does not trace to a requirement entry.

**Decide with.** Apply the [plan ladder questions](../../guides/plan-rules.md#plan-ladder-questions) and the [design comparison](../../guides/plan-rules.md#design-comparison).

**Keeps it when.** A requirement entry fails without the unit, a source or workspace guidance requires it, or a reachable side effect would cause consequential harm.
