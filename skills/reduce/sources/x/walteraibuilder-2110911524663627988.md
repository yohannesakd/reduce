You Suggest. I Build. — Webify Day 2 🚀https://webify.tools

Someone pointed out that I forgot to implement the 🔥 popularity indicator on each tool card.

Before: 🔥 —
Now: 🔥 New

Why "New"? Not enough visits to show meaningful numbers yet.

But here's the funny part:

I asked AI to design this tiny feature.

It came back with an entire analytics architecture. APIs, event tracking, data pipelines... It was halfway to rebuilding GA4. 😂

I stopped it and said:

"Why not just add an <img> tag, return a transparent 1×1 pixel, and count the requests?"

Sometimes AI doesn't make development simpler. It just helps you overengineer faster.

Anyone else spending more time stopping AI from overengineering than actually coding?
